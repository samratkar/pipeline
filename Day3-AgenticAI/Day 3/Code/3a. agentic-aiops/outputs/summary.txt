The paper presents a Semantic SPA (Student-Project Allocation) framework designed to address interoperability challenges in allocating student projects across diverse educational contexts, leveraging Web 3.0 and semantic technologies. Traditional SPA approaches are largely standalone and non-interoperable across institutions, campuses, and disciplines, which becomes problematic in globalization-driven scenarios where expertise and projects may reside outside a single university. The authors argue that existing semantic investments in education—where institutions have exposed data as Linked Open Data and semantically described domain data—can be harnessed to enable situational SPA.

The proposed framework comprises three main components. First, a new OWL ontology called the SPA ontology is introduced to standardize SPA concepts semantically and provide a formal reference for situational SPA data. Second, SOAP-based Web services implement the SPA solution, enabling runtime operations such as querying and matching between students and projects. Third, the OWL-S specification is employed to enable automatic discovery, composition, and invocation of these Web services by educational institutions or semantic agents on the Web, supporting situational SPA scenarios.

By leveraging OWL-S, the framework facilitates automatic service discovery and composition, making SPA services reusable and interoperable across educational boundaries. This aligns with the broader educational shift toward semantic interoperability and data sharing across institutions, campuses, and disciplines, as highlighted in the paper through the context of Web 3.0 and Linked Open Data initiatives.

The paper also situates the Semantic SPA framework within the landscape of higher education needs, pointing to cross-institutional collaboration, interdisciplinary and multi-campus contexts, and cross-disciplinary virtual teams as representative situational scenarios that motivate semantic solutions. The remainder of the work is organized to cover background on semantic Web technologies in education and detailed presentation of the Semantic SPA framework.
